You create a league for one named competition. If it is live, its remaining weeks are your fantasy season. If the next one has not started, you can set up in advance: draft now, score when play begins.

How it plays

01

Snake draft, real pool

Eight rounds, pick clock running, order reverses every round. It is the format fantasy players already know. A final ninth round claims your Squad: one pro org whose own results score for you all season.

02

Five starters, three in reserve

Start a Vanguard, a Duelist, a Strategist, and two Flex picks. Lineups lock when the week's first pro match starts, and carry forward until you change them.

03

Real stats, head-to-head

Eliminations, assists, damage and healing, scored from the published match lines. Every week is a head-to-head matchup; wins and points decide the table. Top four make the playoffs, semifinal winners meet in the final.
