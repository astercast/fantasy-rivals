How the Grand Finals field is set

Twelve teams reach the Grand Finals in Stockholm. Each region's Stage 2 playoff winner qualifies directly. The remaining places go to the top of the annual League Points table, which adds up points from Stage 1, the Mid-Season Finals and Stage 2, with Stage 2 weighted heaviest. The field splits four from the Americas, four from EMEA, two from the Pacific and two from China. That is why the Stage 2 playoffs matter here: they decide the direct qualifiers and most of the points table at the same time.

Scoring

Your players score from their real match stats. Eliminations and assists are worth the most, deaths cost a little, and damage, blocking and healing add up per thousand. When a player's team wins the series, the player gets a win bonus, and a 25-elimination series earns a carry bonus. Your drafted Squad (one pro org) scores its own results: a point per map, two per series, two more for a clean sweep. Every league can tune its own table; this is the standard one, modelled on Fantasy LCS's published table.
